# 📊 Dynamic Excel Dashboards Showcase

A curated portfolio of 6 interactive, business-driven Excel dashboards designed for complex data analysis, monitoring, and decision-making across various industries (Manufacturing, Quality, Compliance, Service Operations, Energy, and Sports).

> ⚠️ **Note on Data Privacy:** Due to non-disclosure and proprietary considerations, the underlying dataset structures represent an anonymized/synthesized blend of real-world operational logic and simulated data. Raw Excel files are retained privately; full visual and architectural breakdowns are presented below.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Excel Methods
- **ETL & Data Cleaning:** Power Query (M Language)
- **Data Modeling:** Power Pivot, Star-Schema relationships, DAX Measures
- **Interactive UI/UX:** Form Controls, Timeline Slicers, Dynamic Array formulas (`INDEX/MATCH`, `XLOOKUP`, `FILTER`), Conditional Rules
- **Visualization:** Pareto analysis, Bullet graphs, Trend analysis, KPI Scorecards
